# Installation and fitment

## Checking that a product fits your vehicle

Enter your vehicle year, make and model in the vehicle selector at the top of any category page. Product pages then show a green Fits badge or a red Does not fit badge. Some vehicles need a dash kit or wiring harness, which the page lists as required accessories.

## Dash kits and wiring harnesses

A dash kit lets a standard head unit sit cleanly in your dashboard opening. A wiring harness connects the new head unit to the factory plug so no wires have to be cut. Both are sold separately and are listed on the head unit page for your vehicle.

## Professional installation

We partner with certified installers in most metro areas. At checkout, enter your zip code to see local installers and prices. Basic head unit installation starts at $99, and amplifier and subwoofer installation starts at $179.

## Installing it yourself

Every head unit includes a printed quick start guide, and installation videos for the most common vehicles are available from the Help Center. Disconnect the negative battery terminal before starting any work.

## Steering wheel controls and backup cameras

Most vehicles keep steering wheel controls with a compatible interface adapter. Backup cameras need a separate camera input, and the product page shows whether the head unit has one.
