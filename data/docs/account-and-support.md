# Account and support

## Resetting your password

Select Sign in, then Forgot password, and enter the email on your account. A reset link arrives within a few minutes and stays valid for 60 minutes. Check the spam folder if it does not arrive.

## Updating your email or address

Open Account settings to change your email, saved addresses and payment methods. Changing the email requires confirming a code sent to the new address.

## Deleting your account

You can request account deletion from Account settings. Order history required for tax and warranty purposes is retained for 7 years in anonymised form, and everything else is removed within 30 days.

## Contacting support

Support is available by email and live chat from 9 am to 8 pm Eastern, Monday to Saturday. Email replies arrive within one business day. Phone support is not offered.

## Business and bulk orders

For orders of 10 or more units, contact the business sales team for volume pricing. Business accounts can also be set up with net 30 payment terms after a credit review.
