# Orders and payments

## Accepted payment methods

We accept Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Discover, PayPal and Apple Pay. Buy now pay later financing is offered through a third-party provider on orders over $200.

## Changing or cancelling an order

An order can be changed or cancelled until it is packed, which is usually within 2 hours of purchase. Contact support right away with the order number. Once an order has shipped it cannot be cancelled, but you can return it.

## Promo codes

Only one promo code can be used per order. Codes cannot be combined with clearance pricing and cannot be applied after the order is placed. If a code is rejected, check its expiry date and the minimum order value in its terms.

## Sales tax

Sales tax is calculated at checkout from the delivery address. Tax exempt customers can upload an exemption certificate in their account before ordering.

## Payment failures

If your card is declined, check the billing address and the card security code first. A pending charge from a failed attempt disappears from your statement within 3 to 5 business days without any action from you.
