# Shipping

## Delivery times

Standard shipping takes 3 to 5 business days within the contiguous United States. Express shipping takes 1 to 2 business days and is available for orders placed before 2 pm Eastern. Orders to Alaska, Hawaii and US territories take 6 to 10 business days.

## Shipping costs

Standard shipping is free on orders over $75. Below that, standard shipping costs $7.95. Express shipping costs a flat $19.95 regardless of order size. Oversized items such as subwoofer enclosures ship with a freight surcharge shown at checkout.

## International shipping

We ship to Canada and Mexico only. International orders are shipped with tracked service and take 7 to 14 business days. The customer is responsible for any import duties and taxes charged on delivery.

## Tracking an order

A tracking number is emailed once the order leaves our warehouse, usually within one business day. You can also open the Orders page in your account and select the order to see the carrier status.

## Delayed or lost packages

If a tracked package shows no movement for 5 business days, contact support with the order number. We will open a carrier investigation and either reship the order or refund it once the carrier confirms the loss.
