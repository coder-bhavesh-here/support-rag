# Returns and refunds

## Return window

Unused items in their original packaging can be returned within 30 days of delivery. Opened electronics can be returned within 30 days only if they are not installed and all accessories are included.

## Items that cannot be returned

Clearance items marked final sale, gift cards, and custom-cut wiring kits cannot be returned. Items damaged during installation are not eligible for a return.

## How to start a return

Open the Orders page, select the order and choose Start a return. You will get a prepaid return label by email. Pack the item with all accessories and drop it off with the carrier named on the label.

## Refund timing

Refunds go back to the original payment method within 5 to 7 business days after the returned item arrives at our warehouse. Original shipping fees are not refunded unless the return is due to our error or a defective item.

## Exchanges

We do not process direct exchanges. Return the original item for a refund and place a new order for the replacement so that it ships sooner.

## Damaged or wrong item received

If your order arrives damaged or you received the wrong item, contact support within 7 days of delivery with photos. We will ship a replacement at no cost or refund the full amount including shipping.
