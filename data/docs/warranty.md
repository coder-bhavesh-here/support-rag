# Warranty

## Standard warranty coverage

Most products carry a one year manufacturer warranty covering defects in materials and workmanship. Speakers and amplifiers from our house brand carry a two year warranty. The warranty starts on the delivery date.

## What the warranty does not cover

Damage from water, overheating, incorrect wiring, power surges, or use beyond the rated power is not covered. Cosmetic wear, torn speaker cones and blown fuses are also excluded.

## Making a warranty claim

Email support with your order number, a short description of the problem and a clear photo or video of the defect. We respond within 2 business days. If the claim is approved we send a prepaid label so you can ship the product for repair or replacement.

## Extended protection plan

You can add a three year protection plan at checkout for 12 percent of the item price. The plan covers accidental damage from handling and adds free return shipping on warranty repairs. It cannot be added after the order ships.

## Warranty and professional installation

Warranty coverage stays valid when the product was installed by a certified installer. Keep the installer invoice, because we may ask for it if a claim involves wiring or power problems.
